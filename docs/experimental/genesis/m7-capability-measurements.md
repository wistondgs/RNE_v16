# M7-E1 — Exact capability measurements

After promotion, the evaluator measures the same `ProgramSkillArtifact` on
HIDDEN, OOD TRANSFER, and three compositional wrappers (`+1`, negation,
absolute value). Each wrapper is identified in its own receipt, preventing an
aggregate result from silently standing in for a different evaluation.

These are exact post-promotion outcomes, not causal attribution. They are the
fixed outcomes that control, treatment, placebo, ablation, and transplant must
compare in the next M7-E causal unit.
