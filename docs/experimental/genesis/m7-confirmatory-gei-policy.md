# M7-G1 — Confirmatory GE-I acceptance policy

`GEI_CONFIRMATORY_BATTERY_V1` is frozen before confirmatory execution. It
requires the exact six canonical tasks and the rule
`ALL_REQUIRED_CAMPAIGNS`; there is no average, substitution, or post-hoc
subset. Each campaign starts from an independent fresh baseline and retention
is explicitly out of scope for this single-capability-run battery.

Earlier `affine-0` evidence remains `PILOT_CAUSAL_ACQUISITION_EVIDENCE` and
is excluded from the confirmatory result. Confirmatory campaigns bind both the
policy and the later battery execution manifest.
